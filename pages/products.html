Selected With Purpose

By keeping our selection intentional, we develop deeper product knowledge, guide clients more effectively and build stronger relationships with the brands we carry.

The Art of Steel

Pellegrin

Italian Precision. Refined.

Pellegrin is a family-owned Italian atelier making thermally broken windows & doors, lift-and-slide, folding and pivot systems.

Why We Chose Pellegrin

35+ years of heritage

Fabricated entirely under one roof in Tuscany

Ultra-thin sightlines

Large openings and custom shapes

Hand-finished welds and seamless corners

Distinctive handmade finishes

Built with profiles from Secco Sistemi, an Italian systems manufacturer with 70+ years of engineering

Explore Pellegrin

Riviera Bronze

American Craftsmanship. Swiss Engineering.

Founded in 2007, Riviera Bronze manufactures thermally broken steel window and door systems in Ventura, California.

Why We Chose Riviera Bronze

American made

Proprietary Swiss profiles developed exclusively for Riviera Bronze

Precision fabrication with traditional, age-old handcrafted putty glazing

Narrow sightlines

Individually created rather than catalog-based

Solid bronze thresholds with client-selected premium hardware

Thermally broken, NFRC-certified systems (National Fenestration Rating Council)

Explore Riviera Bronze

TITONI

How Big Do You Want To Go?

TITONI is a Los Angeles-based manufacturer of thermally broken, high-performance aluminum window and door systems.

Why We Chose TITONI

Monumental openings · expansive glass · remarkably slender sightlines

Fully designed and manufactured in America

Advanced thermal and water-management engineering

Patent-pending Infinity door systems designed to visually disappear

Proprietary hurricane-impact-rated systems

Authorized installation model for greater control and accountability

Explore TITONI

Quartz Luxury by Quaker

Clean Lines. Remarkably Versatile.

From America’s heartland in Freeburg, Missouri, Quartz Luxury offers steel-inspired, thermally advanced aluminum systems.

Why We Chose Quartz Luxury by Quaker

A cost-efficient alternative to steel

Continually evolving product lines

Architectural versatility across traditional, transitional and contemporary design

Custom built for each project

Designed to accommodate substantial openings

70+ years of American manufacturing

Energy-efficient, patented OptiCore® technology — high performance built in

Exceptionally responsive, dedicated regional support where it matters — in the field

Explore Quartz Luxury by Quaker

Sierra Pacific

Deep Roots.

Founded in 1949, Sierra Pacific Industries is a fourth-generation, family-owned forest products company producing custom wood and wood-clad windows and doors.

Why We Chose Sierra Pacific

More than 2.4 million acres across three states of sustainably managed timberland

10+ million trees responsibly planted annually as part of their ongoing forest stewardship

Eight select natural wood options and wood-clad systems offering strong architectural flexibility

Deep control of the material from forest to finished product

Nearly two decades of Cedar Mill Co. experience representing Sierra Pacific, supported by a strong working relationship and local manufacturer support

Explore Sierra Pacific
