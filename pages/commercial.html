Commercial

All Things Glass

Experience That Keeps Projects Moving

Cedar Mill Co.’s dedicated commercial glass division works with general contractors, developers and architects on schools, retail spaces, multi-family, office buildings and hospitality projects. From exterior glazing to interior glass, we bring practical expertise to work where details and deadlines matter. Working closely with our project partners, we anticipate challenges, coordinate installation and keep each stage on track.

Storefront Windows & Doors

Glazing systems for retail, shopping centers, car dealerships and large-scale entrances.

Curtain Walls

Continuous exterior glass façades spanning multiple floors, bringing natural light into the building.

Interior Glass & Railings

Modern glass walls, doors and handrails that define spaces while preserving light and open views.

Specialty Glazing

Hurricane-impact and bullet-resistant solutions designed to help protect both people and property.
